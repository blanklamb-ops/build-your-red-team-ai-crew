# Authorized Use

This tool is for authorized security testing, research, education, and lab use only.

- Use it only with evidence and systems you own or are contractually or legally authorized to assess.
- Do not place real client secrets, personal data, or engagement identifiers in public repositories.
- Treat internal reports as sensitive evidence and follow the engagement's retention and transfer rules.
- Regex redaction is a risk-reduction control, not a guarantee; a qualified operator must review every client draft before release.
