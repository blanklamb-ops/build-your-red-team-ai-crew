# Authorized use

This tool is for **authorized security testing, research, and education only**.

- Use it only on systems and identities you are contractually or legally authorized to test.
- Do not use credential-capture, C2, phishing, destructive, or evasion activity against unauthorized targets.
- The output is advisory and does not establish authorization, safety, stealth, or EDR bypass.
- Keep real commands and resulting evidence in engagement-approved storage.
- Remove client secrets, real victim data, and engagement identifiers before publication.
